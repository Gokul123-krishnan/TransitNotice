TransitNotice – Salem City Bus Transit Update Tracker

A purpose-built, responsive public transportation intelligence portal delivering official service updates, route diversions, and transit maintenance notices for Salem City, Tamil Nadu, India.

Strictly Restricted to Salem City Transit Network
Project Scope & City Exclusivity

Most statewide transit portals mix inter-city express buses with local updates, leaving daily city bus passengers in Salem confused during sudden road diversions or smart city construction works.

TransitNotice is engineered exclusively for Salem City. Cities like Chennai, Coimbatore, and Madurai are intentionally excluded to ensure 100% localized accuracy and high-fidelity monitoring of Salem's intra-city bus network.

Ethical Scraping & Live Verification

TransitNotice adheres to strict data ethics and transparent source attribution guidelines:

  • Robots.txt & Terms Compliance: Scrapers strictly inspect and respect robots.txt crawl policies.
  • Zero Circumvention: We never bypass CAPTCHA, authentication screens, or rate limits.
  • Content Fingerprinting: SHA-256 cryptographic hashing detects modifications, extensions, and restorations.
  • No Fabricated GPS Dots: We strictly avoid fake live GPS map pins that mislead passengers.
  • Asia/Kolkata Timezone: All validity windows, scraper logs, and timestamps use IST (UTC+05:30).
